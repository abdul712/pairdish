## IMPLEMENTATION NOTES

### Publishing Order
1. **First 50:** Chicken + Rice + Middle Eastern + Desserts (highest volume, fastest SEO return)
2. **Next 100:** Lamb, Beef, Snacks, Soups, Breads, Sauces, Breakfast
3. **Last 150:** Global fusion, Seasonal, Roundups, Pillars, Technique

### SEO per Article
- Title: exact keyword match
- URL: `/recipes/{keyword-slug}/`
- Meta description: unique, compelling
- Recipe schema: required (WP Recipe Maker or JSON-LD)
- Images: 3-5 AI-generated (FLUX), 1200x1200px, original
- Internal links: link to 3-5 related recipes + 1 tool page

### Content Quality for Premium Ad Networks
- 800+ words per recipe
- Original ingredient measurements
- Step-by-step instructions (not copied)
- Personal tips / chef notes
- Prep time, cook time, servings, calories
- No source article mentions or URLs
- No "halal" in title (it's an implicit guarantee)

### Image Strategy
- AI-generated via FLUX (Runware setup)
- Use premium food sites as *style reference* only
- Consistent aesthetic across all 300 recipes
- 1 hero image + 2-3 process/cross-section images per recipe
